❤️ Health Monitor for High Altitudes

The smart glasses continuously measure heart rate, blood oxygen (SpO2), and body temperature. If oxygen drops or heat rises, a flaming warning appears on the AR lens, advising rest or hydration.

❤️ Heart Rate

78 bpm

💨 SpO2

96%

🌡️ Temp

36.8°C

In case of abnormal readings, the glasses suggest: “Rest in shade, drink water, or descend to lower altitude.”
